Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

MedGames is an educational collection of short medical mini-games. This policy describes the current prototype version of the app.

Information stored on your device

MedGames stores gameplay statistics such as games played, correct answers, scores, and best streaks locally on your device using Apple’s UserDefaults storage. These statistics are used to show your progress inside the app.

Information we collect

The current version does not collect or transmit personal information. There is no account, sign-in, server connection, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or backend.

Permissions and third parties

The current version does not request access to your photos, camera, microphone, location, contacts, health data, or other device sensors. It does not include in-app purchases or third-party services.

Medical content

MedGames is for education and entertainment only. It is not medical advice and is not a clinical decision-support tool. Prototype content is reviewed separately before release.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to match the released code.
